# 教学过程逐环节评审要求

Use this reference whenever a submitted lesson design contains a teaching-process or activity-design section. The goal is to prevent generic comments about 教学过程.

## Required Process Audit

For complete designs, include a section named **教学过程逐环节评审**. Evaluate each major activity, task, or lesson phase. If there are many small steps, group them into 4-6 meaningful phases.

For each phase, state:

- **环节/任务**: Name the activity or phase.
- **学生要做什么**: Identify the actual student task, not just the teacher action.
- **可见产出**: Identify the observable product, response, model, worksheet, explanation, performance, or decision.
- **学科思维**: Explain the subject-specific thinking being developed.
- **评价证据**: Identify how the design checks whether learning happened.
- **扣分风险与改法**: Name one concrete risk and a targeted revision.

## Depth Rules

- Do not treat “教师讲解、学生思考、小组讨论” as sufficient process quality unless a student output and evaluation criterion are visible.
- For high scores, each objective should be traceable to at least one activity and one process evaluation.
- When digital technology appears, judge whether it changes the learning task: visualization, modeling, feedback, data analysis, collaboration, diagnosis, or creation. Mark decorative use as a risk.
- When course-ideology appears, judge whether it emerges from the activity evidence and subject reasoning, not from a slogan after the fact.

## Scoring Link

The process audit should explain the score for “教学过程/活动设计” in the official 15-point dimension. If the 15-point score is high, cite the strongest phases. If points are deducted, connect the deduction to missing output, weak evaluation, unclear problem progression, decorative technology, or loose subject reasoning.
