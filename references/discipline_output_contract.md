# 教学比赛·教学设计专项评审输出契约

Use this reference after loading the official rubric and the matched subject-stage pattern file. It controls output depth and discipline specificity.

## Required Depth

Every review must show three layers:

1. Official-rubric judgment: score against the 30-point teaching-design rubric.
2. Subject-stage judgment: explain what counts as high-score evidence in this exact 学段+学科.
3. Corpus-strength judgment: state whether the subject-stage pattern is A/B/C sample strength and adjust confidence.

## Score Explanation Rule

For each official dimension, do not only say “较好/不足”. Explain:

- What evidence appears in the submitted design.
- Why that evidence matters in this discipline.
- What is still missing for a top competition score.

## Discipline-Specific Review Modules

Every full review should include these modules unless the user asks for a very short answer:

1. **学段学科识别**: state recognized 学段、学科、basis from the file. If uncertain, say why.
2. **总体竞争力判断**: high/medium/low competitiveness, biggest advantage, biggest scoring risk.
3. **官方教学设计评分表**: 30-point official score with reasons and risks.
4. **学科化高分证据**: compare the submitted design to the matched pattern file.
5. **教学过程逐环节评审**: audit every major activity or task sequence. For each one, identify student task, visible output, subject thinking, evaluation evidence, and risk/revision.
6. **获奖作品画像对照**: use sample strength and topic clusters from `corpus_sample_index.md`; do not quote or copy full sample text.
7. **优先修改清单**: rank by likely score impact, not by writing order.
8. **可直接替换文本**: provide concrete replacement text for objectives, core problem chain, activity-evaluation language, or course-ideology wording.

## Replacement Text Minimum

When a submitted design is complete enough to score, provide at least two replacement blocks:

- A revised core question/problem chain.
- One revised objective, activity-evaluation criterion, or course-ideology paragraph.

## Subject Feature Rule

Use discipline language. Examples:

- Chinese: language practice, textual evidence, reading-writing transfer.
- Math: concept formation, representation conversion, reasoning/proof, variation.
- English: theme meaning, discourse analysis, language scaffolds, communicative output.
- Science/Biology/Chemistry/Physics: phenomenon, evidence, model, experiment, explanation, safety.
- Geography: map/data evidence, spatial scale, regional cognition, human-earth coordination.
- History: source evidence, time-space framework, historical explanation.
- Politics/Morality/Law: issue-based inquiry, normative evidence, public reasoning, value judgment.
- PE/Dance: movement quality, practice density, safety, performance evaluation.
- Art/Music/Calligraphy: visual/musical/calligraphic elements, technique, expression, aesthetic evaluation.
- Psychology: safety boundary, experience, reflection, strategy transfer.
- Information Technology: computational thinking, data/algorithm/system model, debugging, digital ethics.

## Teaching Process Detail Rule

When a design includes teaching activities, process feedback must not be a single summary paragraph. Use the process-audit reference and evaluate activity by activity. At minimum, name:

- The student action and visible product.
- The subject-specific reasoning required.
- The evaluation evidence and whether it matches the objective.
- One precise improvement for that activity.

## Avoid

- Do not praise “设计完整” without naming discipline evidence.
- Do not praise “活动链完整” without explaining what students produce and how that product is evaluated.
- Do not create classroom-performance scores from a teaching design.
- Do not infer missing classroom behavior unless the teaching design explicitly states it.
- Do not treat content domain as an independent subject when the school curriculum has a clearer subject label.
- Do not overstate low-sample conclusions; mark them as cautious pattern evidence.
