# 教学比赛中小学教学设计专项评价标准

Source basis: `教学比赛动员部署会材料0409.pdf`, materials four and five. This reference only covers the **teaching design** part.

## Online Showcase: Teaching Design 30 Points

### 1. 背景分析、学习目标、方法策略: 10 points

Look for:

- 课标分析: related core literacy, curriculum content, and academic requirements are specific and show teacher thinking.
- 教材分析: identifies the unit/theme position, learning content, core literacy elements, course-ideology elements, and teaching focus.
- 学情分析: analyzes student psychology/cognition with evidence and identifies learning difficulty.
- Learning objectives point to core literacy, are based on background analysis, are layered, implementable, and measurable.
- Behavioral/cognitive verbs are accurate and observable.
- Methods and strategies target the teaching focus/difficulty and guide active learning.

Common deduction risks:

- Background analysis copies curriculum standards without converting them into instructional judgment.
- Learning objectives use vague verbs such as “understand/master/cultivate” without measurable student behavior.
- Method strategies are generic and do not address the stated difficulty.

### 2. Problem Framework: 5 points

Look for:

- Core question points to disciplinary core literacy and has openness/exploratory value.
- Core question runs through the lesson and is situated in a problem context.
- Problem chain effectively decomposes the core question and shows the path to solving it.
- Logical structure is clear and matches student cognition.

Common deduction risks:

- Core question is only a knowledge-point title.
- Problem chain is a list of teaching steps rather than thinking progression.
- Questions do not connect to learning activities.

### 3. Teaching Process / Activity Design: 15 points

Look for:

- Overall learning activities are closely related, goal-oriented, logically complete, and suitable for student cognition.
- Activities guide active student learning and exploration.
- Every learning objective has corresponding process evaluation.
- Evaluation method is operational and can reflect goal achievement.
- Design embodies teaching-learning-assessment consistency.
- Support materials effectively support teaching and learning.
- Board design is reasonable, shows key/difficult points, has clear logic, and reflects abstraction/generalization. PE/dance may omit board design.
- Digital technology effectively supports teaching and learning; its use and intent are clear.
- Course-ideology elements are accurately refined and organically integrated.

Common deduction risks:

- Many activities but no core problem line.
- Student tasks are only “think/discuss/answer” without visible output.
- Evaluation only says teacher evaluation/self-evaluation/mutual evaluation without criteria.
- Digital technology is decorative.
- Course ideology is slogan-like.

## Official 30-to-100 Diagnostic Conversion

Use this only for detailed diagnosis, not as the official score:

- Background analysis and student diagnosis: 20
- Learning objectives and core literacy: 15
- Core question and problem chain: 15
- Learning activities and task chain: 25
- Process evaluation and teaching-learning-assessment consistency: 10
- Support materials, digital technology, and course ideology: 15

Report both scores as:

`官方教学设计分: x/30; 诊断折算分: y/100`

## Field Handling

If a submitted design lacks a required template section, score based on actual evidence and flag the missing section. Do not infer intended design from topic alone.
