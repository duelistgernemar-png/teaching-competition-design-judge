# 初中道德与法治教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 20 local designs. Topics include rule of law, law-abiding society, government, administrative organs, obligations, justice, collective growth, emotions, culture confidence, and AI face-swap cases.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior morality-and-law designs use social dilemmas and cases to develop rule consciousness, legal reasoning, responsibility, and value judgment. Strong lessons avoid moral preaching; they make students analyze cases, debate choices, cite norms, and plan actions.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name adolescent cognition, life conflict, and legal/moral misconception.
- Objectives should include concept understanding, case analysis, value judgment, and behavioral choice.
- Core question should be议题式 and arguable: “法治与德治如何协同”, “权利义务怎样统一”.
- Activities should include案例辨析、角色立场、规范链接、观点论证、行动迁移.
- Evaluation should examine reasoning evidence, normative accuracy, and civic action orientation.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for current cases, AI/digital-society scenarios, legal articles or policy materials, debate tasks, and civic participation products.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于教师宣讲、案例分析浅、学生只表态、法理概念不准、评价只看态度。

## Revision Priority

Convert content into one public issue; add normative evidence; require student argument; design action or decision output.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 生活/社会议题、规范依据、价值辨析和行动选择是核心。
- 高分作品常用真实案例推动学生论证权利、义务、规则和责任。
- 扣分最敏感的是说教化、只表态不辨析、法理依据不足。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 面对【真实议题】，我们应依据什么规则作出怎样的选择？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用道德与法治的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从道德与法治学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
