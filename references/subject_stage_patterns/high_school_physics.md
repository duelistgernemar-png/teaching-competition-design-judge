# 高中物理教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium-high, 12 local designs. Topics include rockets, orbiting, buoyancy, work-energy, momentum, thermodynamics, photoelectric effect, centripetal force, mechanical energy, and electric applications.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school physics designs build models from phenomena and evidence. Strong lessons use real scientific/engineering contexts, experiments or simulations, quantitative reasoning, model assumptions, and transfer to new conditions.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify students' intuitive model and mathematical/modeling obstacle.
- Objectives should include modeling, experimental/data reasoning, quantitative analysis, and scientific attitude.
- Core question should require a physical model: “火箭为什么能升空”, “能量如何转化并守恒”.
- Activities should include情境建模、实验/仿真、变量分析、公式推导/解释、迁移应用.
- Evaluation should check model construction, equation meaning, evidence use, and boundary awareness.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for diagrams, graphs, sensors/simulation, derivation tied to phenomenon, and engineering/national science contexts.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于公式灌输、情境与模型无关、实验数据不用、模型条件不讲、课程思政硬贴航天素材。

## Revision Priority

State the model problem; add data/diagram evidence; require students to explain assumptions; include transfer with changed conditions.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 物理情境、变量、实验/数据、模型和公式意义必须贯通。
- 高分作品常用真实现象引出模型，再用实验、图像或定量推理验证。
- 扣分最敏感的是公式先行、实验数据不用、模型条件不清。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样建立模型解释【现象】，这个模型在什么条件下成立？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用物理的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从物理学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
