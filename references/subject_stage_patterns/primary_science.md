# 小学科学教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 17 local designs. Topics include shadows, seasons, plant stems, seed germination, circuits, heat transfer, wind, irrigation systems, rocks and minerals, Antarctica station design, greenhouse agriculture, and project-style science tasks.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary science designs let students investigate a phenomenon and explain it with evidence. The strongest lessons use a puzzling observable event, student prediction, hands-on experiment or observation, recording, comparison, explanation, and everyday application.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify students' prior everyday explanations and the scientific idea to be built.
- Objectives should include observing, measuring, recording, comparing, explaining, designing, or improving.
- Core questions should be investigable: “影子为什么会变”, “怎样让小灯泡亮起来”, “风力如何判断”.
- Activities should have a clear inquiry path and material control.
- Evaluation should check evidence use: whether students can use records to support claims.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for科学记录单, safety reminders, variable control at an age-appropriate level, group roles, and a final claim-evidence explanation. Digital technology is valuable when used for sensors, time-lapse, data visualization, simulation, or documentation.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于实验像演示、学生只看不探究、结论先给出、记录表不能支持解释、材料准备与安全风险不清、项目任务缺少科学概念落点。

## Revision Priority

Make the question experimentally answerable; add prediction-record-explanation; specify materials and safety; use evaluation criteria for evidence quality.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 现象、观察/实验、记录、证据解释和应用迁移是核心。
- 高分作品让学生亲自观察、测量、制作或探究，并用记录支持结论。
- 扣分最敏感的是实验演示化、结论先行、记录单不能支撑解释。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 我们能依据哪些证据解释【科学现象/问题】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用科学的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从科学学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
