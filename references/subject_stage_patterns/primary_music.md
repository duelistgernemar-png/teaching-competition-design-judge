# 小学音乐教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 28 local designs. Topics include Chinese instrumental works, folk songs, regional music, games, rhythm, singing, appreciation, and traditional music culture.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary music designs let students hear, feel, perform, and create with musical elements. Strong lessons avoid turning music into cultural explanation only; they use listening, singing, movement, rhythm, instrument imitation, or creative performance to make melody, rhythm, timbre, tempo, mood, and style perceptible.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' musical experience and the element that is hard to perceive or perform.
- Objectives should include listening discrimination, singing/playing/movement performance, emotional expression, and cultural understanding.
- Core questions should be musical: “旋律怎样表现水乡”, “节奏如何塑造英雄形象”.
- Activities should move from整体聆听 to 要素聚焦 to 表现实践 to 创编/评价.
- Evaluation should use musical criteria: rhythm accuracy, melodic contour, tone, expression, cooperation, and style fit.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for repeated purposeful listening, body movement, graphic notation, layered performance, regional/cultural context, and student-created musical responses.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于知识讲解替代音乐体验、只唱不听、评价没有音乐要素、文化思政脱离作品声音、活动碎片化。

## Revision Priority

Identify one central musical element; design listen-perform-create progression; add musical evaluation language; let culture grow from sound evidence.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 聆听体验、音乐要素、表现实践、创编和审美评价是核心。
- 高分作品让学生通过听唱奏动创感知节奏、旋律、音色、风格。
- 扣分最敏感的是音乐课知识化、文化介绍替代声音体验。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 音乐中的【要素】怎样表现【情绪/形象/风格】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用音乐的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从音乐学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
