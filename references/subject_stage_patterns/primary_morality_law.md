# 小学道德与法治教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium, 10 local designs. Topics include rules, waste, children's day, animals, learning methods, reform and innovation, cultural diversity, heritage, and occasional cross-folder PE material.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary morality-and-law designs start from children's real life and guide them to make value judgments through experience, discussion, and action. They avoid preaching. The lesson should move from a familiar dilemma to rule/virtue recognition, perspective taking, choice reasoning, and a doable behavior commitment.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should describe children's life experience, moral confusion, and age-specific reasoning level.
- Objectives should include recognizing a value/rule, explaining why it matters, and practicing an action.
- Core questions should be dilemma-like: “玩笑什么时候会伤人”, “浪费和我有什么关系”.
- Activities should include case comparison, role experience, discussion, decision cards, or classroom/community action.
- Evaluation should observe reasoning, participation, empathy, and post-lesson action evidence.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for authentic life cases, child voice, action plans, family/community connection, and value formation grounded in experience. Course ideology is often the core, but it must be embodied in behavior rather than slogans.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于教师说教、案例离学生太远、只有表态没有辨析、活动热闹但没有价值澄清、行动承诺不可执行。

## Revision Priority

Replace abstract value statements with a child dilemma; add role/perspective evidence; create one action task; design a simple behavior evaluation tool.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 生活/社会议题、规范依据、价值辨析和行动选择是核心。
- 高分作品常用真实案例推动学生论证权利、义务、规则和责任。
- 扣分最敏感的是说教化、只表态不辨析、法理依据不足。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 面对【真实议题】，我们应依据什么规则作出怎样的选择？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用道德与法治的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从道德与法治学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
