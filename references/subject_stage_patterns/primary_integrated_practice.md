# 小学综合实践教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: low-medium, 5 local designs. Topics include labor tasks, paper mechanisms, nutrition design, intangible cultural heritage, and emergency kit design.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring integrated-practice designs are project-based and action-oriented. Students should investigate a real problem, design a solution, make or implement something, present evidence, and reflect on improvement. The subject value lies in doing, connecting, and solving, not in listening to background knowledge.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify a real campus/home/community need and students' practical ability.
- Objectives should include inquiry, design, making/action, cooperation, reflection, and responsibility.
- Core questions should be project-driving: “怎样设计更合适的急救包/早餐/非遗名片”.
- Activities should include调查、方案、制作/实施、测试、展示、反思.
- Evaluation should include process evidence, product quality, problem fit, cooperation, and improvement.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for task sheets, role division, material/tool safety, user feedback, iteration records, and public presentation.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于活动像手工课、问题来源不真实、调查流于形式、成果评价只看美观、缺少反思改进。

## Revision Priority

Define the real user/problem; add investigation evidence; write product criteria; require one round of testing and revision.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 真实问题、调查设计、制作实施、展示反思和责任担当是核心。
- 高分作品有真实用户或情境，学生经历调查、方案、制作、测试、改进。
- 扣分最敏感的是活动像手工课，问题不真实，成果评价只看美观。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样通过实践方案解决【真实生活问题】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用综合实践的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从综合实践学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
