# 初中语文教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 21 local designs. Topics include classic prose, poetry, novels, explanatory writing, comparative reading, close reading of words, and writing details.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior Chinese designs use a central interpretive problem to guide close reading and expression. The lesson should help students cite textual evidence, analyze language/form, form an interpretation, and transfer a reading or writing method.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name unit task, genre features, and students' interpretive difficulty.
- Objectives should include finding evidence, analyzing words/sentences/structure, explaining effect, and expressing an interpretation.
- Core question should be text-specific and arguable, not a generic comprehension question.
- Activities should include初读建构、细读证据、比较辨析、表达迁移.
- Evaluation should examine evidence quality, reasoning, reading notes, oral/written expression, and method use.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for主问题统领, textual evidence cards, comparative reading, writing micro-task, and board design showing reading path. Course ideology is strongest when emerging from literary image, cultural spirit, or value conflict inside the text.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于教师讲解替代学生解读、问题散、情感升华空泛、写作任务无支架、评价只凭参与度。

## Revision Priority

Sharpen one interpretive problem; add evidence-based student tasks; set criteria for reading conclusion; connect to a small expression product.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 文本证据、语言形式、情感/思想理解和表达迁移必须连成链条。
- 高分作品通常用一个文本矛盾或表达现象统领细读，最终落到朗读、批注、仿写、短评或写作片段。
- 扣分最敏感的是把语文课讲成内容复述或情感灌输，缺少语言实践证据。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 作者/文本是怎样通过【语言形式】表现【意义/情感/人物】的？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用语文的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从语文学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
