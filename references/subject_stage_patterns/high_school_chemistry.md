# 高中化学教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium, 10 local designs. Topics include chemical cells, reaction limits, nitrogen transformation, reaction energy, rice wine/ethanol, hydrogen fuel cells, reaction rate sensors, materials/acid-base-salt review, and flue-gas desulfurization/denitrification.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school chemistry designs connect real chemical problems with evidence reasoning, microstructure, symbolic representation, and social/industrial application. Strong lessons use experiments, data, models, and authentic production/life contexts.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' representational difficulty across macro, micro, symbol, and energy/change perspectives.
- Objectives should include evidence reasoning, model explanation, equation/symbol use, experiment/data interpretation, and responsibility.
- Core question should be chemically generative: “化学反应如何转化为电能”, “条件如何影响限度/速率”.
- Activities should include情境问题、实验/资料证据、微观模型、符号表达、应用决策.
- Evaluation should assess evidence chain, representation conversion, and explanation accuracy.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for sensor data, reaction diagrams, industrial cases, green chemistry, energy/materials perspectives, and safety/waste handling.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于情境新但化学弱、实验只看现象、微观解释缺失、符号表达不规范、社会责任口号化。

## Revision Priority

Clarify the core chemical concept; add macro-micro-symbol conversion; include data/evidence task; connect application to chemical principle.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 宏观现象、微观解释、符号表达和实验安全是核心。
- 高分作品常从生活/工业问题进入，用实验或数据建立证据链。
- 扣分最敏感的是情境热闹但化学概念弱，三重表征断裂。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 如何用证据解释【物质变化/反应规律】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用化学的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从化学学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
