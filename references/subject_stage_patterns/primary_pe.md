# 小学体育教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: very high, 65 local designs. Topics include running, basketball, football, volleyball, jump rope, gymnastics, martial arts, aerobics, throwing/catching, table tennis, badminton, jumping, rolling, and games.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary PE designs build motor skills through safe, dense, playful, and progressive practice. Strong lessons set a clear movement problem, use games or challenges to maintain motivation, and provide layered practice for different ability levels.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify students' motor foundation, coordination/strength difficulty, safety risk, and interest point.
- Objectives should include movement technique, physical fitness, cooperation, rule awareness, and safety behavior.
- Core questions should be skill-based: “怎样起跳更有力”, “如何传接更稳定”.
- Activities should progress from热身激活、技术体验、分解练习、组合应用、游戏/比赛、放松反思.
- Evaluation should include observable movement criteria, practice density, teamwork, and self-monitoring.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for clear organization routes, equipment use, safety protection, group rotation, differentiated challenges, and immediate feedback. PE may omit board design, but the learning route must still be clear.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于运动负荷不明、安全预案不足、游戏与技能目标脱节、评价只看输赢、练习层级不够、等待时间过长。

## Revision Priority

Clarify key movement standard; add progression and safety; design differentiated stations; add quick observable评价口令/量表.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 动作质量、练习密度、分层挑战、安全保护和表现评价是核心。
- 高分作品有清晰动作标准、组织路线、差异化练习和即时反馈。
- 扣分最敏感的是安全与负荷不清、游戏和技能目标脱节。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样改进【关键动作/战术】以提升运动表现？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用体育的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从体育学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
