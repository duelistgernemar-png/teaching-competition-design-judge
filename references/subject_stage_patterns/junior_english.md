# 初中英语教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 23 local designs. Topics include reading, writing, book reports, environmental protection, space, weather, animals, town changes, first-time experiences, and culture.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior English designs integrate theme meaning, discourse analysis, language learning, and communicative output. Reading or listening lessons should not stop at information extraction; students need to analyze text structure/language and use it for speaking or writing.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify language level, discourse difficulty, and theme-value connection.
- Objectives should include understanding content, analyzing discourse/language, using strategies, and producing meaning.
- Core task should be communicative and theme-driven, such as a report, proposal, speech, review, or discussion.
- Activities should move from prediction/input to comprehension to language noticing to output.
- Evaluation should include content, structure, language accuracy, fluency, interaction, and theme understanding.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for语篇研读, mind maps, sentence frames, peer revision, authentic audience, and intercultural reflection.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于阅读题化、语言点割裂、输出任务突然、评价无英语标准、主题意义被中文说教替代。

## Revision Priority

Define one final communicative product; map input activities to that output; add discourse/language scaffolds; include peer/self assessment criteria.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 主题意义、语篇分析、语言支架和真实输出必须一致。
- 高分作品通常有明确交际任务，输入活动服务最终说/写/展示产出。
- 扣分最敏感的是阅读题化、语言点割裂、输出任务无支架。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: How can we use what we learn from the text to 【communicative task】?

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用英语的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从英语学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
