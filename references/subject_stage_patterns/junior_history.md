# 初中历史教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium-high, 17 local designs. Topics include diplomacy, Ming/Qing economy, Hong Kong and Macao, Song economy, reform and opening, ancient foreign relations, technology and exchange, and wartime newspapers.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior history designs guide students from historical materials to historical explanation. Strong lessons use time-space positioning, primary/secondary sources, comparison, causal analysis, and value understanding.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' chronological, causal, or source-reading difficulty.
- Objectives should include reading sources, locating time-space, explaining cause/effect, and forming historical understanding.
- Core question should be historical and interpretive, such as “外交格局如何变化” or “经济发展因何形成”.
- Activities should include史料研读、信息提取、证据归纳、解释建构、现实/价值反思.
- Evaluation should check source use and explanation quality.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for maps/timelines/source cards, comparison tables, multiple perspectives, and board design showing historical development logic.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于故事化讲述、史料只作装饰、结论先行、时间线混乱、家国情怀口号化。

## Revision Priority

Build one source-based inquiry; add timeline/map; require evidence-backed explanation; let value reflection emerge from historical understanding.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 史料证据、时空定位、因果解释和历史评价是核心。
- 高分作品让学生基于材料形成解释，而不是听教师给结论。
- 扣分最敏感的是史料装饰化、结论先行、家国情怀脱离史实。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 基于这些史料，我们如何解释【历史变化/事件】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用历史的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从历史学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
