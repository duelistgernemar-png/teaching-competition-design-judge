# 高中体育教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: very low, 1 local design. Use cautiously and rely on uploaded evidence.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school PE designs emphasize advanced movement quality, tactical understanding, health management, self-monitoring, cooperation, and sportsmanship. Compared with primary/junior PE, students should participate in planning, monitoring, and reflecting on training.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify physical foundation, skill/tactical difficulty, injury risk, and fitness needs.
- Objectives should include sport competence, health behavior, tactical awareness, cooperation, and moral character.
- Core question should name a performance problem.
- Activities should include targeted warm-up, skill/tactical drill, application contest, load monitoring, recovery, and reflection.
- Evaluation should include movement/tactical indicators, effort/load, cooperation, safety, and self-assessment.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for heart-rate/RPE or other load monitoring, differentiated training, tactical decision tasks, safety plans, and long-term health awareness.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于只练技能、运动负荷不可控、安全不足、评价只看比赛结果、健康行为目标虚化。

## Revision Priority

Define performance indicators; add load/self-monitoring; include tactical or fitness decision-making; strengthen safety and differentiation.

## 获奖作品画像强化模块

Sample level: C 低样本; local sample count: 1.

### Representative Topic Cluster

- 刘欣-刘欣—教学比赛教学设计

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 动作质量、练习密度、分层挑战、安全保护和表现评价是核心。
- 高分作品有清晰动作标准、组织路线、差异化练习和即时反馈。
- 扣分最敏感的是安全与负荷不清、游戏和技能目标脱节。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样改进【关键动作/战术】以提升运动表现？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用体育的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从体育学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
