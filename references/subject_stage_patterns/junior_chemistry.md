# 初中化学教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium-low, 6 local designs. Topics include chloride detection, effervescent tablets, traditional medicine ingredients, chemical micro-projects, and some AI/cross-disciplinary entries that should be re-identified from text.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior chemistry designs connect observable experiments with micro-level explanation and symbol expression. Strong designs use real products or problems, safe experiments, evidence reasoning, and application to life or production.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' macro-only thinking and symbol/micro explanation difficulty.
- Objectives should include experiment observation, evidence analysis, micro explanation, symbol use, and safety awareness.
- Core question should be problem-based: “怎样证明某离子存在”, “泡腾片为什么会产生气体”.
- Activities should include实验方案、现象记录、证据判断、微观解释、应用改进.
- Evaluation should include safety, evidence chain, representation conversion, and explanation accuracy.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for macro-micro-symbol links, safe reagent handling, comparison experiments, product design, and green chemistry/social responsibility.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于实验像魔术、现象与结论跳跃、符号表达缺失、安全规范不足、跨学科任务冲淡化学概念。

## Revision Priority

Clarify the chemical concept; add evidence table; require micro/symbol explanation; write safety and waste-disposal details.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 宏观现象、微观解释、符号表达和实验安全是核心。
- 高分作品常从生活/工业问题进入，用实验或数据建立证据链。
- 扣分最敏感的是情境热闹但化学概念弱，三重表征断裂。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 如何用证据解释【物质变化/反应规律】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用化学的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从化学学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
