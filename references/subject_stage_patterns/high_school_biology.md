# 高中生物教学设计专项评审模式

Sample basis: 12 high-school biology winning teaching designs in the local corpus. Topics include DNA structure, gene expression, transcription/translation, gene mutation, epigenetics, polyploid breeding, protein engineering, and relationship between genes and traits.

## High-Score Focus

High-school biology designs are judged by whether students move from **life phenomenon to biological mechanism** through evidence and models.

## Subject-Specific Rubric Translation

### Background Analysis

High score:

- Locates the lesson in a unit big idea such as genetic information flow, gene expression regulation, variation and evolution, structure-function view, or technology and society.
- Identifies concrete student difficulties: invisible molecular processes, directionality of information flow, confusion between mutation/epigenetics/chromosomal variation, weak evidence chain.
- Connects curriculum standards to biological core literacy: life concept, scientific thinking, scientific inquiry, social responsibility.

Low score:

- Only lists curriculum standards.
- Says students are “weak/interested” without naming cognitive obstacles.

### Objectives

High score objectives ask students to:

- Explain mechanisms using evidence.
- Construct or revise models.
- Analyze experimental/data materials.
- Apply mechanisms to real biological/medical/agricultural situations.

Avoid vague verbs such as “understand” unless paired with observable behavior.

### Core Question and Problem Chain

Strong core questions:

- How does genetic information guide protein synthesis?
- Why can epigenetic changes affect traits without changing DNA sequence?
- How does chromosome number variation create seedless watermelon?
- How do gene expression differences produce phenotype differences?

Strong problem chains move through:

1. Phenomenon/case.
2. Evidence or data.
3. Mechanism/model.
4. Application/explanation.
5. Reflection on technology/society/ethics.

### Activities

High-score activities include:

- Analyzing experimental evidence or data tables.
- Building transcription/translation/expression/regulation models.
- Drawing mechanism diagrams.
- Comparing normal and abnormal cases.
- Explaining a new phenomenon using the model.

Weak activities:

- Watching animation then filling definitions.
- Group discussion without concrete output.
- Teacher completes the mechanism explanation.

### Evaluation

Good process evaluation checks:

- Whether the model is accurate.
- Whether evidence supports conclusion.
- Whether biological terminology is precise.
- Whether students can transfer the mechanism to a new case.

### Digital Technology

High-value uses:

- Visualizing molecular processes.
- Supporting model construction.
- Comparing sequence/expression/data changes.
- Displaying and diagnosing student-generated models.

Low-value uses:

- Playing an animation without student reasoning.

### Course Ideology

Good sources:

- Scientific spirit and evidence-based inquiry.
- Health literacy and life responsibility.
- Agricultural innovation and biotechnology.
- Bioethics, gene technology, biological safety.

Avoid slogans detached from biology.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 生命现象、结构功能、证据推理、模型解释和社会责任是核心。
- 高分作品常从真实生命现象进入，通过资料、模型或实验解释机制。
- 扣分最敏感的是知识讲授化，结构与功能、现象与机制割裂。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样用生物学证据解释【生命现象/机制】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用生物的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从生物学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
