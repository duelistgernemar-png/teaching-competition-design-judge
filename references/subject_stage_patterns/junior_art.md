# 初中美术教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: no local confirmed sample after corpus correction. Use this as a cautious discipline orientation for uploaded junior art designs, not as a winning-sample generalization.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior art designs emphasize visual culture, formal analysis, technique exploration, creative expression, and reflective critique. Compared with primary art, junior lessons should give more space to design intention, cultural interpretation, creative decision-making, and revision.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify students' visual experience, aesthetic misconception, and technique/design difficulty.
- Objectives should include analyzing visual language, applying method, expressing theme, and evaluating/revising work.
- Core question should point to visual decision-making, such as how form, color, material, composition, or style serves expression.
- Activities should include作品观察、形式分析、方法探究、创作实践、展示批评.
- Evaluation should include formal language, technique, originality, theme fit, process evidence, and revision evidence.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for artwork comparison, student sketches, process documentation, material/tool exploration, cultural/design context, critique language, and a final visual work with criteria.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于范作复制、技法步骤替代审美判断、评价只说“好看”、文化解读空泛、创作任务与核心问题脱节。

## Revision Priority

Verify the subject first; then define the visual problem, add creation criteria, require process evidence, and make revision part of the learning task.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 视觉观察、形式语言、技法实践、创意表达和展示评价是核心。
- 高分作品从作品/文化/设计问题进入，学生有草图、创作和评价证据。
- 扣分最敏感的是范作复制、只讲文化不创作、评价只说好看。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样运用【视觉元素/技法】表达【主题/审美意图】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用美术的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从美术学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
