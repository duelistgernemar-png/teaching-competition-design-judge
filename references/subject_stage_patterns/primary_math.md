# 小学数学教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: very high, 81 local designs. Topics cluster around number meaning, area/geometry, statistics, reasoning games, fractions, multiplication, units of measure, graphing, and problem solving.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary mathematics designs help children move from concrete experience to mathematical representation. Strong lessons use objects, drawings, number lines, tables, formulas, and language to make students' thinking visible. They usually include a real or playful task, hands-on exploration, comparison of methods, abstraction of a rule, and a return to application.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify the precise conceptual obstacle, such as confusing area with perimeter, treating fractions as parts only, or memorizing formulas without understanding.
- Objectives should name mathematical thinking: measure, classify, represent, infer, compare, explain, model, verify.
- Core questions should reveal concept formation: “怎样判断面积大小”, “为什么这种算法成立”, “如何用图表示数量关系”.
- Activities should move through操作体验、表征转换、交流辨析、归纳建模、变式应用.
- Evaluation should examine reasoning process, representation quality, and explanation clarity, not just answer correctness.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for multiple representations, purposeful mistakes, student method comparison, and board design that abstracts a mathematical structure. Good digital technology supports dynamic visualization, data collection, or instant feedback rather than decorative slides.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于生活情境热闹但数学问题不清、活动只操作不抽象、评价只看对错、方法灌输、练习堆叠、缺少低龄学生语言支架。

## Revision Priority

Add a concept-conflict task first; then design representation conversion; then write evaluation criteria for “说清理由”; finally add one transfer problem that checks whether students really formed the concept.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 概念形成、表征转换、推理说明和变式迁移是核心证据。
- 高分作品常从冲突情境或典型例证出发，让学生经历猜想、验证、抽象、应用。
- 扣分最敏感的是直接给公式法则、只做例题、评价只看答案。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 为什么【概念/公式/方法】成立？它能解决哪一类问题？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用数学的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从数学学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
