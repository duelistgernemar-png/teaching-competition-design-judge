# 高中历史教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium, 10 local designs. Topics include colonial system, global routes, world trade, cultural exchange, national movements, cultural heritage, ancient wars, and Chinese nation formation.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school history designs emphasize historical interpretation, source-based reasoning, time-space structure, and global/national perspective. Strong lessons frame a historiographical or causal problem and let students use sources to build explanations.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify students' difficulty in macro chronology, causality, or multi-perspective interpretation.
- Objectives should include source analysis, time-space positioning, causal explanation, comparison, and historical value understanding.
- Core question should be interpretive: “全球航路开辟如何重塑世界联系”.
- Activities should include史料组阅读、地图/时间线、观点比较、因果机制建构、历史评价.
- Evaluation should assess evidence selection, explanation logic, and historical concepts.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for diverse sources, maps, timelines, historiographical tension, student argument writing, and value reflection grounded in evidence.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于宏大叙事无学生证据、材料堆砌、结论先行、情怀脱离史实、评价只背知识点。

## Revision Priority

Make one historical problem explicit; select fewer but stronger sources; require written historical explanation; tie values to evidence.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 史料证据、时空定位、因果解释和历史评价是核心。
- 高分作品让学生基于材料形成解释，而不是听教师给结论。
- 扣分最敏感的是史料装饰化、结论先行、家国情怀脱离史实。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 基于这些史料，我们如何解释【历史变化/事件】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用历史的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从历史学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
