# 高中语文教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 27 local designs. Topics include Lu Xun, classical prose, poetry appreciation, comparative reading, novel object/plot analysis, argumentative writing, architecture prose, and group reading.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school Chinese designs center on sophisticated textual interpretation and expression. Strong lessons frame an arguable主问题, guide students to read language/form/context closely, construct evidence-based interpretation, and transfer to writing, criticism, or cultural reflection.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name curriculum task group, genre, unit position, and students' interpretive barrier.
- Objectives should include textual evidence, language/form analysis, logical expression, aesthetic/cultural understanding, and transfer.
- Core question should be deep and text-specific, such as resolving a contradiction in wording, image, plot, or argument.
- Activities should include独立批注、证据交流、比较辨析、观点建构、写作/表达迁移.
- Evaluation should assess evidence sufficiency, reasoning depth, expression quality, and method transfer.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for comparative reading, subtle word analysis, historical/cultural context used as evidence, argumentative writing scaffolds, and board design showing interpretive logic.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于教师阐释过多、主问题虚大、文化升华脱离文本、写作训练无评价量规、学生产出不可见。

## Revision Priority

Sharpen the interpretive conflict; require student evidence; add one written output with criteria; make course ideology grow from textual understanding.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 文本证据、语言形式、情感/思想理解和表达迁移必须连成链条。
- 高分作品通常用一个文本矛盾或表达现象统领细读，最终落到朗读、批注、仿写、短评或写作片段。
- 扣分最敏感的是把语文课讲成内容复述或情感灌输，缺少语言实践证据。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 作者/文本是怎样通过【语言形式】表现【意义/情感/人物】的？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用语文的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从语文学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
