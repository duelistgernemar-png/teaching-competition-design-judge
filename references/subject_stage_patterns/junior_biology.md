# 初中生物教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium, 11 local designs. Topics include blood vessels, seed germination, aquatic animals, natural selection, urine formation, myopia, heart, body boundaries, and plant observation.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring junior biology designs help students explain life phenomena through structure-function, evidence, and health/ecology application. Strong lessons use observation, model, experiment, data, or case analysis to construct biological concepts.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify everyday misconceptions about body/plant/animal phenomena.
- Objectives should include observing, describing structure, explaining function, analyzing evidence, and applying to health/ecology.
- Core question should be life-phenomenon based: “血管结构如何适应功能”, “尿液形成说明了什么”.
- Activities should include观察/实验/资料分析、模型建构、解释表达、生活应用.
- Evaluation should check whether students can use biological evidence to explain and decide.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for diagrams/models, comparison tables, health education grounded in science, and student explanation products.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于知识点讲授化、实验观察无记录、结构和功能割裂、健康建议缺科学依据、评价只背概念。

## Revision Priority

Start from a phenomenon; add evidence/model task; ask students to explain structure-function relation; include application with scientific reasoning.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 生命现象、结构功能、证据推理、模型解释和社会责任是核心。
- 高分作品常从真实生命现象进入，通过资料、模型或实验解释机制。
- 扣分最敏感的是知识讲授化，结构与功能、现象与机制割裂。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样用生物学证据解释【生命现象/机制】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用生物的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从生物学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
