# 高中思想政治教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: medium-high, 14 local designs. Topics include grassroots democracy, property rights, traditional ideas, whole-process people's democracy, fair justice, legal system, intellectual property, economic system, and people's congress system.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school politics designs use议题式教学 to connect theory, real cases, institutional logic, and public reasoning. Strong lessons require students to analyze social phenomena with concepts, evidence, and value judgment.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' life experience, abstract theory difficulty, and misconception.
- Objectives should include concept understanding, case analysis, argumentation, political/legal/economic identification, and public participation.
- Core issue should be authentic and debatable: “制度优势如何转化为治理效能”.
- Activities should include情境材料、理论工具、观点辨析、证据论证、行动/方案表达.
- Evaluation should assess concept accuracy, reasoning, evidence use, and value orientation.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for current public cases, local governance examples, legal/economic materials, debate or proposal tasks, and discipline-grounded course ideology.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于理论宣讲、案例只装饰、学生只表态、概念不准、课程思政与学理割裂。

## Revision Priority

Frame one issue; choose cases that require theory; design argument output; write criteria for concept-evidence-value alignment.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 议题式教学、理论工具、现实材料、公共论证和价值引领是核心。
- 高分作品用社会现象激活理论，让学生用概念解释制度、治理或经济生活。
- 扣分最敏感的是理论宣讲、案例装饰、价值引领与学理分析脱节。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 如何运用【理论/制度】解释并解决【现实议题】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用思想政治的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从思想政治学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
