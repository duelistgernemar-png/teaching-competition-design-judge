# 高中英语教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 25 local designs. Topics include reading, writing workshops, survey reports, active learning, roots and shoots, festivals, avatars, scientific breakthroughs, art, poetry, and cultural themes.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school English designs integrate thematic meaning, discourse analysis, language competence, cultural awareness, and thinking quality. Strong lessons use authentic texts to support advanced output: reports, speeches, debates, proposals, reviews, or reflective writing.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should identify discourse difficulty, language gap, and theme-thinking value.
- Objectives should include comprehension, discourse/language analysis, strategy use, critical thinking, and communicative output.
- Core task should be meaningful and audience-aware.
- Activities should progress from theme prediction and reading/listening to language noticing, thinking discussion, and output revision.
- Evaluation should include content depth, organization, language accuracy/richness, interaction, and cultural awareness.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for genre scaffolds, discourse maps, lexical/grammar resources for output, peer review, and authentic communicative purpose.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于语篇浅层问答、主题意义中文化口号化、输出无支架、评价只有语言准确性或只有态度。

## Revision Priority

Define the final genre and audience; map text analysis to output needs; add language bank and peer revision criteria; include critical/cultural reflection.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 主题意义、语篇分析、语言支架和真实输出必须一致。
- 高分作品通常有明确交际任务，输入活动服务最终说/写/展示产出。
- 扣分最敏感的是阅读题化、语言点割裂、输出任务无支架。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: How can we use what we learn from the text to 【communicative task】?

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用英语的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从英语学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
