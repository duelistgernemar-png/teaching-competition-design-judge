# 高中数学教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 24 local designs. Topics include trigonometry, sequences, probability, Bayes, binomial distribution, complex numbers, derivatives, solid geometry, vectors, variance, and modeling applications.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school mathematics designs foreground mathematical abstraction, logical reasoning, modeling, and transfer. Strong lessons let students experience why a theorem/model/formula is needed, derive or justify it, and apply it to non-routine situations.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name prerequisite structures and high-order thinking difficulty.
- Objectives should include abstraction, reasoning/proof, operation, modeling, and application.
- Core question should reveal a mathematical need or contradiction.
- Activities should include问题情境、探索猜想、推理论证、方法结构化、变式/模型应用.
- Evaluation should examine reasoning chain, representation, model selection, and transfer.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for dynamic visualization, proof scaffolds, real data/modeling, multiple approaches, and board design that shows mathematical structure.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于例题驱动代替问题驱动、只训练技巧、证明过程教师包办、信息技术炫技、评价只看算对。

## Revision Priority

Set one structure-generating problem; add student derivation/proof; include non-routine transfer; assess explanation and modeling, not only result.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 概念形成、表征转换、推理说明和变式迁移是核心证据。
- 高分作品常从冲突情境或典型例证出发，让学生经历猜想、验证、抽象、应用。
- 扣分最敏感的是直接给公式法则、只做例题、评价只看答案。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 为什么【概念/公式/方法】成立？它能解决哪一类问题？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用数学的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从数学学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
