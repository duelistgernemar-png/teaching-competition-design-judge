# 小学书法教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: low, 2 local designs. Topics use characters such as “守” and “永”, combining stroke/structure learning with cultural meaning.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary calligraphy designs combine character observation, brush method, structural awareness, repeated practice, feedback, and cultural understanding. A strong lesson turns one character into a learning object: students see form, understand rule, practice deliberately, and express value.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' writing habit, brush/pen control difficulty, and character-structure obstacle.
- Objectives should include observing stroke features, applying writing method, improving one work through feedback, and understanding cultural meaning.
- Core questions should connect form and spirit: “怎样写出这个字的稳与势”.
- Activities should include范字观察、要点提炼、临写、对照修改、展示评价.
- Evaluation should include stroke quality, structure, rhythm, posture, and improvement evidence.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for enlarged model characters, comparison before/after, teacher demonstration checkpoints, peer feedback language, and culture-integrated interpretation of the character.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于只讲文化不练字、只临摹无观察、评价笼统、缺少姿势和工具规范、低样本结论被过度泛化。

## Revision Priority

Make the writing difficulty explicit; add visual comparison; require one revised work; connect cultural meaning to the character form.

## 获奖作品画像强化模块

Sample level: C 低样本; local sample count: 2.

### Representative Topic Cluster

- 张洪帅-《寸土不让——“守”》教学设计
- 周奥-《矢志不渝·永》教学设计

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 范字观察、笔法结构、临写修正和文化审美是核心。
- 高分作品让学生看见字形规律，经历临写、对照、修改和展示。
- 扣分最敏感的是只讲文化不练字、只临摹无观察和修正。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样通过【笔画/结构】写出【字的神采/文化意味】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用书法的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从书法学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
