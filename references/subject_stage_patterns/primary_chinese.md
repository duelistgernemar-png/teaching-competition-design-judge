# 小学语文教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 125 local designs. Typical topics include ancient poetry, low-grade literacy and reading, fairy tales, narrative prose, classic reading, observation writing, and unit reading-writing integration.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary Chinese designs turn text learning into visible language practice. The lesson usually starts from a child-friendly reading puzzle, then leads students through reading aloud, word and sentence tasting, image reconstruction, character or emotion understanding, and a small transfer task. The strongest designs do not stop at “understanding content”; they show how students learn language through朗读、圈画、比较、补白、复述、仿写、表演 or oral expression.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name the unit language element, grade-level literacy difficulty, and students' reading/expressive starting point.
- Objectives should be observable: read with tone, identify key words, explain expression effect, retell with sequence, write a sentence/paragraph using a learned method.
- The core question should point to language and meaning, such as “作者怎样把变化写出来” rather than only “课文讲了什么”.
- Activities need a progression from整体感知 to 细读品味 to 语言积累/表达迁移.
- Evaluation should use朗读表现、批注质量、表达作品、同伴反馈 as evidence.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for child experience, repeated close reading, language-form awareness, and a final language product. Excellent course-ideology integration grows from text emotion, cultural image, family/country feeling, or reading habit, not from detached slogans.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于目标空泛、问题停留在内容问答、朗读无评价标准、写作迁移突然出现、低年级活动成人化、板书只列情节不显语言方法。

## Revision Priority

First clarify the language element; then rewrite the core question; then add one visible student product with criteria; finally connect朗读/批注/表达 evidence to each objective.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 文本证据、语言形式、情感/思想理解和表达迁移必须连成链条。
- 高分作品通常用一个文本矛盾或表达现象统领细读，最终落到朗读、批注、仿写、短评或写作片段。
- 扣分最敏感的是把语文课讲成内容复述或情感灌输，缺少语言实践证据。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 作者/文本是怎样通过【语言形式】表现【意义/情感/人物】的？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用语文的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从语文学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
