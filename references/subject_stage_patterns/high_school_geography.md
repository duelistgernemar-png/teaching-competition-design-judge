# 高中地理教学设计专项评审模式

Sample basis: 18 high-school geography winning teaching designs in the local corpus. Topics include service industry location, agricultural/industrial location, industrial transfer, regional coordination, regional cognition, Arctic region, Southeast Asia, Saudi Arabia, and research travel from a geographic perspective.

## High-Score Focus

High-school geography designs are judged by whether students use **map/data evidence and regional thinking** to complete spatial analysis, causal explanation, and decision/evaluation.

## Subject-Specific Rubric Translation

### Background Analysis

High score:

- Connects curriculum standards, unit position, and student difficulty to a real geographic problem.
- Identifies core literacy: regional cognition, comprehensive thinking, human-earth coordination, geographic practice.
- Names concrete student difficulties: listing factors without weighing them, weak map interpretation, poor scale switching, shallow causal explanation.

Low score:

- Treats geography as social-topic discussion without spatial evidence.
- Lists location factors but does not analyze dominance/change.

### Objectives

High score objectives ask students to:

- Read and extract map/data information.
- Analyze spatial distribution and location factors.
- Explain regional differences or change mechanisms.
- Evaluate development decisions or propose location/coordination plans.

### Core Question and Problem Chain

Strong core questions:

- Why is this service/industry located here?
- How do location factors change over time?
- How does industrial transfer affect regional development?
- How should a region coordinate development under given natural and human conditions?

Strong problem chains:

1. Where is the phenomenon?
2. What spatial pattern is shown?
3. Which factors explain it?
4. Which factor is dominant and why?
5. What regional impact or decision follows?

### Activities

High-score activities include:

- Map reading and spatial positioning.
- Data/table/chart interpretation.
- Region comparison.
- Location factor weighting.
- Drawing factor relationship diagrams or transfer path maps.
- Making development recommendations with evidence.

Weak activities:

- Case storytelling without map/data reasoning.
- Students list factors but do not compare or evaluate.
- Technology replaces geographic thinking.

### Evaluation

Good process evaluation checks:

- Accuracy of map/data extraction.
- Completeness of factor analysis.
- Ability to identify dominant factors.
- Quality of region-based explanation or recommendation.

### Digital Technology

High-value uses:

- Map overlay and spatial comparison.
- Data visualization.
- Route/region simulation.
- Displaying student analysis products.

Low-value uses:

- Decorative maps or videos.

### Course Ideology

Good sources:

- Regional coordinated development.
- National strategy.
- Rural revitalization.
- Ecological civilization.
- Industrial upgrading.
- Cultural confidence through regional cases.

For any map use, flag the need to follow public map content requirements.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 地图/数据证据、空间分布、尺度转换、区域解释和人地关系是核心。
- 高分作品常用真实区域任务，让学生读图析因并作出决策。
- 扣分最敏感的是地图装饰化、因素罗列无权重、缺少空间证据。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 为什么【地理事象】在这里这样分布/变化？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用地理的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从地理学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
