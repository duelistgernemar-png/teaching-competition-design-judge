# 小学英语教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 35 local designs. Topics include colors, food, pets, seasons, weather, body parts, directions, clubs, weekly plans, and lower-grade story/dialogue lessons.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary English designs build language through meaningful situations and repeated, supported output. The lesson should not be a word-sentence drill sequence; it should give children a reason to listen, speak, read, write, choose, and share. Strong designs use songs, pictures, stories, role play, information gaps, mini surveys, posters, or show-and-tell tasks.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' known language, likely pronunciation/meaning difficulties, and age-appropriate interests.
- Objectives should separate language knowledge, communicative function, learning strategy, and cultural awareness.
- The core question/task should be communicative, such as planning breakfast, introducing a pet, giving directions, or sharing a club.
- Activities should move from input comprehension to controlled practice to semi-open or open output.
- Evaluation should include pronunciation, accuracy, fluency, meaning completion, cooperation, and audience awareness.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for情境连续性, sentence scaffolds, vocabulary recycling, multimodal input, and a final communicative product. Course ideology is strongest when embedded in healthy habits, care for animals, respect for difference, family/community life, or cultural comparison.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于中文讲解过多、活动只跟读替换、输出无真实听众、评价没有语言标准、文化意识贴标签、低年级书写负担过重。

## Revision Priority

Rewrite the lesson as one communicative mission; add sentence frames and choice space; make the final output observable; add simple child-friendly criteria.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 主题意义、语篇分析、语言支架和真实输出必须一致。
- 高分作品通常有明确交际任务，输入活动服务最终说/写/展示产出。
- 扣分最敏感的是阅读题化、语言点割裂、输出任务无支架。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: How can we use what we learn from the text to 【communicative task】?

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用英语的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从英语学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
