# 高中心理健康教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: low-medium, 5 local designs, with one likely cross-folder politics item. Topics include personality growth, procrastination, happiness, gender impressions, and self-development.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring high-school psychology designs support self-understanding, emotion/stress management, identity, relationships, and life planning through safe reflective practice. They should respect autonomy and avoid pathologizing or exposing students.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name developmental pressure and psychological safety needs.
- Objectives should include awareness, cognitive/emotional reframing, strategy practice, and action planning.
- Core question should be self-development oriented: “我如何与拖延相处”, “性别印象如何限制选择”.
- Activities should include体验/测评慎用、情境讨论、方法建构、练习迁移、个人计划.
- Evaluation should assess reflection depth and strategy application, not judge personality.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for confidentiality boundaries, non-invasive activities, cognitive tools, peer support norms, and after-class action plans.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于心理测评滥用、公开隐私、价值说教、缺少策略训练、活动可能引发不安全感。

## Revision Priority

Add ethical safety rules; use scenarios instead of forced disclosure; teach one usable psychological tool; require a private action plan.

## 获奖作品画像强化模块

Sample level: C 低样本; local sample count: 5.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 心理安全、体验觉察、策略练习和迁移应用是核心。
- 高分作品用低风险情境帮助学生认识自我、练习方法、形成行动计划。
- 扣分最敏感的是公开隐私、德育说教化、活动情绪风险过高。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 面对【心理/人际/成长情境】，我可以怎样觉察并调适？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用心理健康的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从心理健康学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
