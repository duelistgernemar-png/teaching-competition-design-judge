# 小学美术教学设计深度模式

Sample strength: high, 33 local designs. Topics include folk crafts, birds, windows, animation, ceramics, flags, cultural heritage, Beijing gifts, decorative tiger heads, spatial/visual design, and occasional cross-disciplinary entries.

## Winning Orientation

High-scoring primary art designs balance visual appreciation, technique learning, creative expression, and exhibition evaluation. The best lessons do not merely teach a craft procedure; they let students observe visual language, understand cultural or design meaning, then create a personal work with criteria.

## Judge Focus

- Background analysis should name students' visual experience, technique difficulty, and creative barrier.
- Objectives should include observing, analyzing form/color/texture/composition, using a method, and expressing an idea.
- Core questions should be visual: “怎样用纹样表达祝福”, “色彩如何形成窗户的美感”.
- Activities should move through欣赏观察、技法示范/探究、创作实践、展示评价.
- Evaluation should include visual features, craftsmanship, originality, and expression fit.

## High-Score Evidence

Look for high-quality image resources, comparison of artworks, technique checkpoints, student design sketches, iterative improvement, and exhibition language. Course ideology works well through traditional culture, local heritage, labor, community, or aesthetic confidence.

## Deduction Risks

扣分常见于范作复制、技法步骤过细而创意不足、欣赏与创作断裂、评价只说好看、材料安全与工具管理不清。

## Revision Priority

Add a visual-analysis task; clarify technique and creative choice; build a small rubric; ensure every child has space for individual expression.

### Discipline-Specific Scoring Differentiators

- 视觉观察、形式语言、技法实践、创意表达和展示评价是核心。
- 高分作品从作品/文化/设计问题进入，学生有草图、创作和评价证据。
- 扣分最敏感的是范作复制、只讲文化不创作、评价只说好看。
- 评分时必须说明该教学设计的学生产出是什么，以及产出如何证明本学科核心素养达成。

### High-Score Structure To Look For

1. 从真实情境或学科现象提出核心问题。
2. 用递进问题链组织学生经历“感知/观察/阅读/体验 - 分析/探究/练习 - 表达/解释/创作/应用”。
3. 设置可见学生成果，并给出分层评价标准。
4. 支撑材料、数字技术和课程思政都服务本学科关键学习困难。

### Replacement Text Templates

Core question: 怎样运用【视觉元素/技法】表达【主题/审美意图】？

Objective template: 学生能够基于【材料/现象/文本/任务】，运用美术的【关键方法】，完成【可见成果】，并说明【证据/理由/改进依据】。

Evaluation criterion template: 能否使用【本学科证据】支撑【解释/表达/作品/动作/方案】，标准分为“不能说明 - 能部分说明 - 能完整说明 - 能迁移或改进说明”。

Course-ideology template: 本课的价值引领应从美术学习过程自然生成，落在【尊重证据/审美判断/规则意识/文化理解/健康责任/数字伦理/公共参与】中的一个重点，避免泛化口号。
